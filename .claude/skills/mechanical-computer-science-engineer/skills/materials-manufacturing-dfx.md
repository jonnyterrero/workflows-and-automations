# Materials, Manufacturing, and DFX

## Material selection

Choose materials based on:
- stiffness
- strength
- fatigue
- toughness
- hardness
- wear
- density
- corrosion
- temperature
- manufacturability
- cost
- availability

Do not choose a material based on ultimate tensile strength alone.

## Manufacturing-aware design

For machined parts:
- tool access
- internal radii
- setup count
- tolerances
- stock size
- surface finish

For additive manufacturing:
- orientation
- anisotropy
- support requirements
- wall thickness
- overhangs
- dimensional compensation

For molded parts:
- draft
- wall uniformity
- ribs
- bosses
- sink
- parting line

## Tolerances

Use tolerances based on function.

For fits, specify whether the intent is:
- clearance
- transition
- interference

Perform tolerance stack-up for assemblies where multiple dimensions determine fit or alignment.

## DFX

Review:
- DFM
- DFA
- DFS
- DFT
- DFR
- cost
