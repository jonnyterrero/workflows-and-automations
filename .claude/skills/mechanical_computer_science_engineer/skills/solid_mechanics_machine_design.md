# Solid Mechanics + Machine Design

## Stress and strain

Normal stress:

\[
\sigma=\frac{F}{A}
\]

Average shear stress:

\[
\tau=\frac{V}{A}
\]

Axial deformation:

\[
\delta=\frac{FL}{AE}
\]

Beam bending:

\[
\sigma=\frac{My}{I}
\]

Torsion for circular shafts:

\[
\tau=\frac{Tr}{J}
\]

## Design workflow

1. Define load cases.
2. Calculate reactions.
3. Determine internal forces/moments.
4. Determine stress field.
5. Determine deformation.
6. Check static failure.
7. Check buckling where relevant.
8. Check fatigue.
9. Check wear/contact.
10. Apply safety factor based on uncertainty and consequence.

## Fatigue

Do not treat fatigue as a static yield check.

Consider:
- mean stress
- alternating stress
- stress concentration
- surface finish
- size effects
- reliability
- notch sensitivity
- load spectrum

Use Goodman, Gerber, Soderberg, or appropriate fatigue models as justified.

## Machine elements

For shafts, bearings, bolts, gears, springs, and joints:
- calculate loads
- determine stress
- calculate life or capacity
- check deflection/alignment
- choose standard sizes
- verify manufacturability
