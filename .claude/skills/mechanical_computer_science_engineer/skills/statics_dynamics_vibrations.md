# Statics, Dynamics, and Vibrations

## Statics

Always begin with a free-body diagram.

Use equilibrium:

\[
\sum F_x=0,\quad \sum F_y=0,\quad \sum M=0
\]

For 3D systems use all six scalar equilibrium equations where independent.

## Dynamics

Select the most efficient framework:
- Newton-Euler
- work-energy
- impulse-momentum

For translation:

\[
\sum \vec F=m\vec a
\]

For rotation about a fixed axis:

\[
\sum M=I\alpha
\]

## Vibrations

For a single-degree-of-freedom system:

\[
m\ddot{x}+c\dot{x}+kx=F(t)
\]

Natural frequency:

\[
\omega_n=\sqrt{\frac{k}{m}}
\]

Damping ratio:

\[
\zeta=\frac{c}{2\sqrt{km}}
\]

Always identify whether excitation frequency approaches resonance.

## Required checks

- sign convention
- coordinate system
- constraints
- degrees of freedom
- energy consistency
- limiting cases
